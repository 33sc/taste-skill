Questions before you book

Something else? Harry is on 07700 900123.

How long can the scaffold stay up?

As long as your quote says. It lists the hire weeks and what an extra week costs, so a builder running late is no surprise bill.

Does scaffolding on the pavement need a licence?

Yes, from the council. We apply for you; most need 10 working days’ notice, and the fee is on your quote.

Is your scaffolding work insured?

Yes, fully. We send a copy of the certificate whenever you ask, with your quote on a commercial job.

Is a scaffold a security risk?

Tell your home insurer before it goes up. Each evening we take the bottom ladder away or lock it off.

Which towns in Hampshire do you cover?

All of it. Fareham is ten minutes away; Portsmouth, Southampton and Winchester about half an hour; Basingstoke and the New Forest too.
