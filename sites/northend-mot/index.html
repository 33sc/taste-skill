Your MOT, start to finish

Bring the car with its keys, the locking wheel nut key and some fuel in the tank. See the opening hours

  1. Tested while you work

    Drop the keys in the morning on your way to work. We ring you as soon as the test is done.

  2. We show you, you decide

    Fails and advisories in plain English, the worn part in front of you. Nothing extra is done until you say yes.

  3. Fixed here, retested free

    Leave the car with us for the repairs and the partial retest within 10 working days is free, under DVSA rules.
